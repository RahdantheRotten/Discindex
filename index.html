Welcome to Discindex

A free, simple way to organize your CD collection.

  • 📷 Scan a barcode with your phone or webcam, paste a Discogs link, or search by name
  • 💿 Browse your albums as a cover grid or list, or every song you own
  • 🔍 Search any column: artist, year, label, even song length
  • ⬇ Import your Discogs collection in one click

Your collection is saved privately in your own browser. Album info comes from Discogs; you'll need a free Discogs account and token.

Settings

Discogs connection

Your personal token is saved only in this browser. Get one at discogs.com/settings/developers.

Import from Discogs

Copies every item in your Discogs collection into Discindex. Discogs only allows about one release per second, so a big collection takes a few minutes. Items you already have are skipped, so you can run it again later to pick up new additions.

Backup

Save your collection as a file, or load one back.
